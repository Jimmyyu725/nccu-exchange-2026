Beautiful 2-bedroom apartment located near the Muzha MRT / Zoo, in the heart of Wenshan District in Taipei City (15 minute drive from Taipei City Hall). The luscious greenery of the Wenshan wildlife reserve directly behind the apartment's floor-to-ceiling glass windows will immediately ease you into vacation mode, as you enjoy the apartment's state-of-the-art amenities, including a 50" TV, Bose surround sound system, and in-room projector. Your best choice for a relaxing getaway in Taipei!

The space
The building itself is a high-end residential complex with 24/7 doorman security. An elevator can take you from the basement parking garage to the first floor and to the apartment (8F). The apartment comes with an open kitchen, luxurious living room, two bedrooms, two full baths, a balcony with a washer/dryer unit, as well as central AC throughout the entire unit, controllable via a universal remote, and WIFI. Guests will also have access to two parking spaces in the basement parking garage.
